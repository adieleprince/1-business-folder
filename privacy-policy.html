Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Royal Dynasty Fragrance ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Order information: your name, phone number, email address, and delivery address, collected when you place an order.
  • Account information: if you create an account, your username, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Payment-related information: for card and bank payments, your payment is processed directly by our payment partner, Paystack — we do not receive or store your full card details. For Ghana Mobile Money payments, you upload a payment receipt/screenshot for our team to manually verify.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address, if you choose to subscribe.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including sending order confirmations and status updates.
  • Verify payments submitted through Ghana Mobile Money.
  • Respond to questions or requests you send us.
  • Send occasional updates to newsletter subscribers.
  • Maintain and improve this website.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only where necessary to operate the store:

  • Paystack — to process card and bank payments.
  • Resend — to deliver transactional emails, such as order confirmations and status updates.
  • Delivery partners — your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with the delivery company handling your order.
  • We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember your shopping cart and, if you have an account, to keep you signed in. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy — for example, to process your order or maintain your account — unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to ask about, update, or request removal of your personal information, including asking to be removed from our newsletter list.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
